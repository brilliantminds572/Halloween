How to choose Halloween decorations

Start with lighting. Flickering LED candles, pumpkin string lights and glowing props set the mood fast and are safer than open flames. Then add texture with spider webs and skeletons for doorways, porches and mantels. Battery-powered items are the easiest to place anywhere.

Easy Halloween costume ideas for kids and adults

Classic looks like witches, vampires and skeletons are quick to put together and work for school parties, trick-or-treating and office events. Glow-in-the-dark pieces are a good pick for evening walks because they make kids easier to spot.

Spooky science fun for curious kids

Halloween is a great excuse for hands-on STEM. Potion kits, glowing crystals and skeleton models turn the holiday into a mini lab and keep kids busy for an afternoon.
